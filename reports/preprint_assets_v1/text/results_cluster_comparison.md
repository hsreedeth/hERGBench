On the frozen cluster_seed11 split, the Stage 1 ECFP4 + XGBoost baseline achieved AUROC 0.772, AUPRC 0.838, F1 0.732, balanced accuracy 0.620, and Brier 0.215. The final Stage 2 ChemProp D-MPNN cluster benchmark used calibration.method = none and was repeated across 5 torch seeds on the same fixed split membership; the repeated runs yielded AUROC 0.780 +/- 0.010, AUPRC 0.825 +/- 0.007, F1 0.761 +/- 0.109, balanced accuracy 0.710 +/- 0.022, and Brier 0.244 +/- 0.007. Relative to the frozen Stage 1 baseline, the repeated Stage 2 cluster runs showed a modest AUROC gain and higher mean balanced accuracy and F1, while AUPRC was slightly lower and Brier score was higher. The final Stage 2 cluster policy uses calibration.method = none, which was retained after the cluster-only calibration ablation showed that platt produced an implausibly aggressive operating point and isotonic materially altered ranking behavior.
